Shoulda Eaten Dinner — every sound the game can play

Grouped by the moment it happens in, in rough play order.

How to use this page

  1. Work down one section at a time. Play every clip in it back to back — that is roughly what a player hears during that moment, because the game picks at random from the set and never repeats the same clip twice in a row.
  2. Write down the filenames you want cut. Cutting a clip is one line deleted from game/scripts/AudioCasting.gd — the line that names that file. Nothing else has to change, and no test checks how many clips a set has.
  3. Want a clip to play at a different moment instead? Note the filename and the moment. Moving it is the same one line, pasted into a different set.
  4. Watch for the coloured tags. placeholder means no family recording exists yet for that moment — the game is playing a stand-in. CC0 stock means it is a free download, not your family. not currently triggered means the sound is loaded but nothing in the game plays it today.
  5. Nothing here plays automatically and nothing loads until you press play, so the page opens instantly.

If a player shows as unsupported, open this page in Chrome — some browsers are fussy about .ogg files.

Worth knowing for both door sets: in play the door itself takes over the pitch and loudness the moment the clip starts, based on how fast it is actually swinging.
